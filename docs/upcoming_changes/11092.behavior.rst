- ``DynamicFactorMQResults.news`` with ``state_index="common"``
  used the first ``k_states - k_endog`` states, which are the
  factor states only for monthly data with idiosyncratic AR(1)
  terms. In other models, they included idiosyncratic states or
  left out factor states, or ``news`` raised an ``IndexError``.
  ``"common"`` selects the factor states (the factors and their
  lags), so the impacts and weights of the news with
  ``state_index="common"`` change for all other models.
