- ``x13_arima_analysis`` and ``x13_arima_select_order`` raised a ``TypeError`` for a pandas ``endog`` without dates in
  its index, such as a RangeIndex, even with ``start`` and ``freq``.
  They use ``start`` and ``freq`` as documented, and raise the
  ``ValueError`` of array_like input when either is missing.
