- The penalty of ``GEE.qic``, and so the QIC of ``GEE`` results, used a
  model-based information without the inverse of the variance function of the
  family, which is right only for the Gaussian family. The QIC of results of
  other families, such as the Binomial and the Poisson, changes; the
  quasi-likelihood and ``qicu`` do not. :pr:`11034`
