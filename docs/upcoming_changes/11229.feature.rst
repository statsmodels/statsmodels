- :func:`~statsmodels.tsa.arima.estimators.innovations.innovations` takes
  the keyword-only arguments ``ar_order`` and ``m`` and estimates ARMA
  models by the innovations algorithm of Brockwell and Davis (2016),
  section 5.1.3. ``m`` is the recursion level, by default 17 for models
  with autoregressive terms. ``ARIMA.fit(method="innovations")`` and
  ``gls(arma_estimator="innovations")`` accept models with autoregressive
  terms. Estimates for pure moving average models are unchanged.
  :issue:`6159`, :pr:`11229`
