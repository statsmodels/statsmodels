- The EM steps of :class:`~statsmodels.tsa.regime_switching.markov_regression.MarkovRegression` and :class:`~statsmodels.tsa.regime_switching.markov_autoregression.MarkovAutoregression` (``em_iter`` and ``search_iter``
  of ``fit``, which by default starts from 5 EM iterations) divided the
  smoothed joint probabilities of the transition probabilities by the
  smoothed probabilities of the current regime instead of the previous
  one, so the estimated transition probabilities were off by about
  1/nobs. The parameters after the EM steps, and the estimates that
  ``fit`` reaches from them, change, mostly slightly; for a short series
  ``fit`` can stop at another local maximum. :pr:`11066`
