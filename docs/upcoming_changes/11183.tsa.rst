- ``untransform_params`` of the Markov switching models
  (:class:`~statsmodels.tsa.regime_switching.markov_regression.MarkovRegression`,
  :class:`~statsmodels.tsa.regime_switching.markov_autoregression.MarkovAutoregression`)
  with three or more regimes raised "Could not untransform parameters."
  for transition probabilities that are zero or nearly zero, so that
  ``fit`` raised when the EM steps (``em_iter``) drove one to zero. It
  uses the closed-form inverse of the logistic transform, with zero
  probabilities replaced by the smallest positive normal float.
  Negative or NaN probabilities, or probabilities out of a regime that
  sum to more than one, raise a ``ValueError`` for any number of
  regimes. :pr:`11183`
