- With ``em_initialization=False``, ``DynamicFactorMQ.fit`` and
  ``fit_em`` switch every block of factors with non-stationary
  parameters to a diffuse initialization and restart. In models of
  monthly data without idiosyncratic AR(1) terms, only the first
  block of factors was checked, and only with a global
  initialization, so a non-stationary block could keep its
  stationary initialization (with a loglikelihood of 0, after which
  the EM iterations stopped at the apparent decrease of the
  loglikelihood); fits of such models with several blocks of
  factors change. With idiosyncratic AR(1) terms or quarterly
  variables, a global initialization (for example
  ``initialization="stationary"``) raised a ``KeyError``.
  :pr:`11093`
- With ``transformed=False`` and ``em_initialization=False``,
  ``DynamicFactorMQ.fit`` and ``fit_em`` restarted the EM iterations
  (after switching states to a diffuse initialization) from the
  start parameters transformed a second time. They restart from the
  start parameters transformed once, so these fits change.
  :pr:`11093`
