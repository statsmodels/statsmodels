- ``get_prediction``, ``get_forecast``, ``forecast`` and ``predict``
  of state space models (``SARIMAX`` and ``VARMAX``) added the
  defaults of the extended model, such as its trend offset, to the
  ``extend_kwargs`` dict passed to them. Reusing that dict with another
  model, or with the same model after ``append`` or ``extend``, gave
  wrong forecasts for models with a time trend. The dict is no longer
  changed. :pr:`11169`
