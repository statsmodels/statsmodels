- With ``em_initialization=False``, ``DynamicFactorMQ.fit`` and
  ``fit_em`` did not detect a negative or complex eigenvalue outside
  the unit circle in the transition of a block of factors or of the
  block of quarterly idiosyncratic AR(1) terms after an EM step (the
  eigenvalues, not their moduli, were compared with one), so the block
  kept its stationary initialization (with a loglikelihood of 0, after
  which the EM iterations stopped at the apparent decrease of the
  loglikelihood) instead of being switched to a diffuse initialization
  with a restart. Fits of such models change. :pr:`11120`
- After such a restart, ``DynamicFactorMQ.fit`` and ``fit_em``
  replaced the model's initialization by the default initialization,
  and changed an initialization set by block in place (the switched
  states were diffuse). The model keeps its initialization,
  unchanged. :pr:`11120`
