- The ``results``, ``run_filter`` and ``prefix`` arguments of ``smooth``
  of ``KalmanSmoother`` have no effect and are deprecated; they will be removed after
  statsmodels 0.17 is released. :pr:`11044`
- A ``smooth_method`` given to ``smooth`` of ``KalmanSmoother`` that cannot be used
  with the filter method (univariate smoothing without univariate
  filtering, or the reverse) is ignored, as before, with a
  ``FutureWarning``; it will raise a ``ValueError`` after statsmodels
  0.17 is released. :pr:`11044`
