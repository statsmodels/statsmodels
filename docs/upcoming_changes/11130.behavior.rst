- The summary of state space results with ``cov_type="robust_approx"``
  (and ``"robust"`` or ``"robust_oim"``) decides whether to warn that the
  covariance matrix is singular from the rank of the robust covariance
  matrix, not from that of the outer product of gradients matrix. The
  warning can now appear (or disappear) for such results; the covariance
  matrices and standard errors do not change. :pr:`11130`
