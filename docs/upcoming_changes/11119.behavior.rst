- ``get_impacts`` of the news of state space models (``NewsResults``)
  with ``groupby`` given as a dict dropped the impacts of the grouped
  revisions (the rows "all prior revisions", for the revisions before
  ``revisions_details_start``) unless the dict mapped "all prior
  revisions". They are a group of their own, so such grouped tables
  gain a row with the impacts of these revisions. :pr:`11119`
