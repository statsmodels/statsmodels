- Without a formula, ``wald_test_terms`` of models that name their
  parameters (state space models such as ``SARIMAX`` and
  ``UnobservedComponents``, and Markov switching models) used the
  positions of the exog columns as the positions of the parameters:
  the tests were labelled with the exog names, and were the tests of
  other parameters when the coefficients of exog were not the first
  parameters (for example, the test of the intercept of
  ``SARIMAX(..., trend="c")`` was labelled with the name of exog).
  Models without exog raised a ``TypeError``. The table has one test
  per parameter, labelled with ``param_names``. Regression, GLM and
  discrete models are not affected. :pr:`11160`
