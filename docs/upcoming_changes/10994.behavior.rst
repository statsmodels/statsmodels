- With ``switching_variance=True`` and ``order`` of 2 or more, :class:`~statsmodels.tsa.regime_switching.markov_autoregression.MarkovAutoregression`
  used the error variance of the regime ``order - 1`` periods earlier
  instead of that of the current regime, so the log-likelihood, the
  estimates and the regime probabilities were wrong. Results of such
  models change. :pr:`10994`
