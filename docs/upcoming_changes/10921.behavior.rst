- Since statsmodels 0.15.0, in-sample quarter strings such as
  ``"2001Q2"`` as the ``start`` or ``end`` of a prediction selected the
  last day of the quarter on daily and monthly date indexes, the wrong
  quarter on quarter-start indexes, and only the first month on monthly
  period indexes. They select the whole quarter again, as in 0.14, so
  predictions with these keys change. :pr:`10921`
