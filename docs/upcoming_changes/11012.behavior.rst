- ``predict`` of :class:`~statsmodels.tsa.regime_switching.markov_autoregression.MarkovAutoregression` and of ``MarkovSwitchingResults`` with ``conditional`` True or 1
  to ``order`` (conditional on the current regime and fewer than
  ``order`` past regimes) returned the predictions multiplied by the
  probability of the conditioning regimes. They are the expected values
  given those regimes, and NaN where that probability is zero, so
  results change. :pr:`11012`
