- ``MixedLMResults.bse_re`` and the standard errors of the random effects
  variances, covariances and variance components in
  ``MixedLMResults.summary`` were off by about a factor of the square root of
  ``scale``, because they were computed for these parameters divided by
  ``scale``. They are now the standard errors of ``cov_re`` and ``vcomp``,
  include the uncertainty of ``scale``, and change with the square of the
  units of the dependent variable. For the ``dietox`` random intercept model
  in the documentation, the standard error of the group variance is 6.94
  instead of 2.15. The standard errors of the fixed effects do not change.
  :issue:`2231`, :pr:`11013`
