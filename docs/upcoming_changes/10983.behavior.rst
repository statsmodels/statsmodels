- The log-likelihood of :class:`~statsmodels.tsa.vector_ar.svar_model.SVAR` was wrong for a ``B`` that is not
  triangular, and the identification (rank) check rejected identified
  models and accepted unidentified ones. Results for such models
  change. :pr:`10983`
- :meth:`~statsmodels.tsa.vector_ar.svar_model.SVAR.score` is computed
  analytically. It used forward differences whose error exceeded the
  tolerance of the solvers of ``fit``: the default ``"bfgs"`` solver could
  warn that the optimization did not converge, depending on rounding, and
  ``"newton"`` stopped about 3e-5 (relative) from the maximum. The estimates
  of ``fit`` change slightly. :pr:`10983`
