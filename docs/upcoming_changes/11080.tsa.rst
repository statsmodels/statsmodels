- ``summary_impacts`` and ``summary`` of the news of state space
  models (``NewsResults``) showed ``NaT`` in place of the repeated
  impact dates of multivariate models, and raised a ``TypeError`` on
  pandas 3 (a ``FutureWarning`` on pandas 2.x) for data with integer
  dates (given as a list or an ndarray). The repeated dates are
  blank. :pr:`11080`
- ``summary_impacts`` and ``summary`` of the news of state space
  models raised an ``AttributeError`` for a single impact date with a
  single impacted variable (for univariate models whose series has a
  one-letter name, for any single impact date). They show the rows of
  that date and variable. :pr:`11080`
- ``summary_impacts`` of the news of state space models did not
  sparsify the first column for ``groupby="impact_date"`` and
  ``"impacted_variable"``, and its title joined a single impacted
  variable or impact date with "for" in every case. Every spelling
  gives the same table, and the title says "Impacts on [impacted
  variable = ...]" when grouping by impact date (the default) and
  "Impacts for [impact date = ...]" when grouping by impacted
  variable. :pr:`11080`
- ``summary_details`` of the news of state space models with
  ``multiple_tables=True`` and ``groupby="impact date"`` or
  ``"impacted variable"`` raised a ``KeyError``, and so did
  ``summary(details_groupby="impact date")`` (or ``"impacted
  variable"``) of multivariate models. They give one table for each
  impact date or impacted variable. :pr:`11080`
