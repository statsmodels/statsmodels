- :class:`~statsmodels.genmod.generalized_linear_model.GLM` and
  :class:`~statsmodels.genmod.generalized_estimating_equations.GEE` issue a
  ``DomainWarning`` if ``endog`` is outside of the support of the family:
  proportions in [0, 1] for ``Binomial``, a nonnegative ``endog`` for
  ``Poisson`` and ``NegativeBinomial``, and a positive one for ``Gamma`` and
  ``InverseGaussian``. Such data, for example counts without ``n_trials`` in a
  ``Binomial`` model, were fit without a warning. The results are unchanged.
  :issue:`9058`, :pr:`11195`
