- The exact diffuse smoother of state space models used the
  transition matrix of period t instead of t-1 in its backward time
  step. With a time-varying transition matrix and an exact diffuse
  initialization, the smoothed state, its covariance matrix and
  autocovariance, and the smoothed disturbances were wrong in every
  diffuse period but the last. Models with a time-invariant
  transition matrix are not affected. :pr:`11151`
