- ``summary_impacts``, ``summary_details`` and ``summary`` of the news
  of state space models (``NewsResults``) raised a ``KeyError`` for an
  impact date, impacted variable, update date or updated variable
  whose impacts were all within the tolerance (for example the last
  observed date of the previous data, or any date with a large
  ``tolerance``), although a range of such dates gave a table without
  rows. Such a selection gives a table without rows, and a list with
  such a date gives the rows of the other dates. :pr:`11118`
